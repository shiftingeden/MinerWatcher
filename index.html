Frequently asked

  • My miners aren't showing up. Make sure your Mac is on the same local network as your miners, and allow Local Network access when macOS prompts (or enable Miner Watcher under System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local Network). Then use Scan to find them automatically, or add a miner by its IP address.
  • Which devices are supported? Bitaxe and NerdAxe / NerdQAxe miners running the AxeOS API, Avalon Nano miners, and Antminer (Bitmain) miners — including those running Vnish, Braiins OS, or LuxOS — all over your local network.
  • Do alerts work when I'm not looking at the app? Yes. Miner Watcher keeps checking your miners and sending alerts the whole time it is running, including in the background — you don't need a window open, but quitting the app stops monitoring. Make sure Notifications are enabled for Miner Watcher in System Settings, and consider launching it at login (in the app's Controls tab) so monitoring resumes on its own.
  • Can I control my miners, not just watch them? Yes — reboot, switch pool, set fan or performance/work mode, and tune frequency/voltage on supported hardware, per device or across the whole fleet.
  • Does Miner Watcher collect my data? No. It works entirely on your local network with no account and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
